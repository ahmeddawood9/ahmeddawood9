## About

Backend engineer who owns the infrastructure his services run on — and the reliability of it once it's there.

I build APIs in **Java / Spring Boot** and **Python / FastAPI**, provision them on **AWS** with **Terraform**, and ship them through **Docker** onto **Linux** boxes behind **Nginx**. From there it's the operational work that interests me most: pipelines that deploy without drama, metrics and logs that tell the truth, alerts that fire for the right reasons, and runbooks for when they do.

Working toward the overlap of backend, DevOps, and SRE — writing the service, running it, and keeping it honest in production.
